GUEST INFORMATIONS

Everything you need to know

  1. Accommodation

    Where to Stay

    A limited number of rooms have been reserved for our guests at The Alder Hotel, approximately fifteen minutes from the wedding venue. Use the booking code YARA & KAREEM when reserving your room to receive the preferred wedding rate. Please book by July 12, 2027, as availability cannot be guaranteed after this date.

  2. Dress Code

    What to wear

    We invite our guests to wear formal attire with a relaxed garden feel. Suits, formal separates, midi dresses and full-length dresses are all welcome. Part of the celebration will take place outdoors on grass, so block heels or comfortable formal footwear are recommended

  3. Food and Drinks

    Dinner and Refreshments

    A seated dinner, dessert and a selection of alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks will be served throughout the evening. Vegetarian and allergy-friendly options will be available. Please share any dietary requirements when completing your RSVP.

  4. Transportation

    Getting to the Venue

    Complimentary shuttle transportation will be available between The Alder Hotel and The Willow Estate. The shuttle will leave the hotel at 3:00 PM and return trips will begin at 9:30 PM. The final shuttle will leave the venue shortly after 11:00 PM.
